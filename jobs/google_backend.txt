Google - Senior Backend Software Engineer, Distributed Systems

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Backend Software Engineer to join our Core Distributed Systems team. In this role, you will design and build high-throughput, low-latency microservices and event streaming pipelines powering millions of queries per second.

Key Responsibilities:
- Architect scalable backend microservices using Java, Spring Boot, or C++.
- Design fault-tolerant event streaming systems using Apache Kafka and distributed caching layer (Redis).
- Optimize low-latency gRPC services and inter-service communication.
- Collaborate with cloud infrastructure teams deploying microservices on Kubernetes (EKS / GKE).

Qualifications & Skills:
- 3+ years of experience building production backend systems.
- Strong proficiency in Java, Spring Boot, C++, or Python.
- Hands-on experience with Apache Kafka, Redis, gRPC, and Docker/Kubernetes.
- Experience with AWS or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
- BS/MS in Computer Science or related technical field.
