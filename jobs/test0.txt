GreenLeaf Creative Agency - Senior Motion Graphics Designer, Brand & Visual Media

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Motion Graphics Designer to join our Brand & Visual Media team. In this role, you will create high-quality animated content, visual storytelling assets, and branded digital experiences across marketing campaigns, social media platforms, and multimedia channels.

Key Responsibilities:

Create motion graphics, animations, and visual assets for digital marketing campaigns.
Develop creative concepts and storyboards for video production projects.
Produce animated advertisements, promotional videos, and brand content.
Collaborate with creative directors, photographers, and marketing teams to deliver visual campaigns.
Edit and enhance video content using professional post-production workflows.
Manage creative assets, project timelines, and revisions throughout production cycles.
Maintain brand consistency across all visual communication materials.

Qualifications & Skills:

5+ years of experience in motion graphics design, animation, or digital media production.
Strong proficiency in Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Illustrator, and Cinema 4D.
Experience with 2D and 3D animation techniques.
Knowledge of typography, color theory, visual composition, and storytelling principles.
Experience creating content for social media platforms and advertising campaigns.
Familiarity with video production workflows, rendering processes, and creative asset management.
Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, Animation, Visual Communication, or related creative field.
